Questions about notes and meetings

Is a missing note a softer note?

Uncheck one Four note and play a finite measure. If you want a visual answer first, inspect the slot where that note used to arrive.

Read an answer

It is a rest. The removed voice does not sound at that entrance, and the remaining notes are not amplified to compensate. At a shared entrance, removing Four can leave Three there without making the slot silent.

Can fewer occupied slots still contain the same notes?

Compare the middle Three note with two Four answers at offsets zero and one. Read note count and occupied slots separately.

Read an answer

Offset zero has three notes at three slots. Offset one makes one shared entrance: three notes now occupy two slots. Both voices still sound at that meeting; moving an entrance does not delete a note.

Must a complete Four voice meet the last Three call?

Keep only the last Three note and all four Four notes. Compare offsets zero, one and two.

Read an answer

Only offset two meets that last call, at displayed slot 9. Four remains complete in every comparison. Note count alone does not say where the entrances coincide.
